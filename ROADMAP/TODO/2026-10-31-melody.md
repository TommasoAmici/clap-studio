# Melodic sequencer bugs

If I draw notes at a low pitch (e.g. C2) they don't play at all.
Moreover, if I click the piano roll on notes at a low pitch they play with a
noticeable delay. This doesn't happen at higher pitches.

I don't quite understand the `plays at <note>` input either. I think we should
get rid of it and allow users to multiselect notes in the sequencer and move them
up or down in pitch using the mouse or arrow keys.
